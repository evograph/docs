# Evograph identity

The mark is a branching evolution trail: one starting point and three connected outcomes. It also suggests a lowercase e. The original geometry is shared by the website, favicon, and exported vectors.

Use the horizontal wordmark where space allows, the stacked lockup in a square composition, and the mark alone for small icons. SVG wordmarks contain outlined DM Sans glyphs and do not require installed fonts. The exports include green, single-color ink, and reversed paper versions. Use reversed artwork on evergreen or a similarly dark background.

Palette: paper #F4F1E9, ink #20251F, evergreen #264C3B, clay #A44C31. Typography: DM Sans for reading and headings; IBM Plex Mono for commands, IDs, and annotations. Fonts are self-hosted through Fontsource and licensed under the SIL Open Font License; licenses are included in the font packages.

Keep clear space at least equal to one node diameter. Minimum mark size: 24px; minimum horizontal lockup width: 140px. Do not distort the paths, add shadows, apply gradients, or place the green mark on a low-contrast background. The name and identity identify the Evograph project; they do not imply endorsement by another vendor.

Regenerate with `npm run generate:brand`. Source: scripts/generate-brand.mjs. Social image: og.png, 1200×630. App icons: 180, 192, 512px; favicon uses a 32px PNG inside ICO.
